# Pay2GPT Connect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Pay2GPT Connect reads the ChatGPT Session of the account signed in to chatgpt.com in your browser, only when you click
**Connect ChatGPT in one click** on a pay2gpt.com page or **Get Session** in the extension's popup.

**What it does with it**

- Hands it to the pay2gpt.com page that asked for it, which sends it to your order so Pay2GPT can open your subscription.
  How Pay2GPT handles it from there is described in the [Pay2GPT privacy policy](https://pay2gpt.com/privacy).
- Or, from the popup, copies it to your clipboard or saves it as a file you choose to send to @Pay2GPTBot.

**What it does not do**

- It stores nothing: no Session, no history, no settings.
- It sends nothing to any server of its own or to any third party, and collects no analytics.
- It does not read any site other than chatgpt.com, and runs on no page other than pay2gpt.com and ru.pay2gpt.com.
- It does not use or transfer data for advertising, credit or any purpose unrelated to connecting your order.

Contact: support@pay2gpt.com
